TaskFlow has its own internal mail between TaskFlow users. It does not send to outside email addresses.

1. Open **Mail** (under Communication Management) and click **Compose**.
2. Add recipients in **To** (and **Cc** if needed) by typing a name or email: recipients must be active TaskFlow users.
3. Write the subject and message. You can attach files (up to 5 files, 10 MB each, 25 MB in total).
4. Click **Send**, or **Save draft** to finish later.

I can also draft a mail for you: tell me who to write to and what it is about, review the draft I show you, and click **Send mail** to confirm. Nothing is sent until you confirm.
