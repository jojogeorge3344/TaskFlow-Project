The bell in the header shows how many notifications are unread. Open **Notifications** (under Communication Management) to read them.

- You are notified about new assignments, reassignments, status changes, comments and deadline reminders.
- Click a notification to open the task it is about, and mark notifications as read when you have seen them.
- Deadline reminders also arrive in your Mail inbox from "TaskFlow" when that is switched on.
