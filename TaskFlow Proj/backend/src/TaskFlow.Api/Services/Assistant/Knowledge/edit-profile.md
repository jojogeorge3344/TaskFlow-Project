You can change your own **full name** and **email** on **My Profile**.

1. Open **My Profile**.
2. Under **Account details**, edit your name or email.
3. Click **Save changes**.

Your role, project, employee code and username cannot be changed here. They are managed by an Administrator in Employee Management.

I can also make this change for you: tell me the new name or email, review what I show you and confirm.
