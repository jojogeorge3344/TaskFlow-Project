Open **Tasks** (Task Management, or My Tasks for employees).

- Use the search box to find a task by title or task code.
- Filter by **Status**, **Priority**, assignee (for Managers and Administrators) and deadline range.
- Click a task to see its details, change its status, comment or view its history.

Employees see the tasks they are assigned to, created or are notified about. Managers and Administrators see all tasks.
