You can change a task's status from the task page or from the Board.

**From the task page**
1. Open **Tasks** (Task Management or My Tasks) and click the task.
2. In the side panel, under **Move to**, click the status you want.

**From the Board**
1. Open **Board**.
2. Drag the task card into the column for its new status.

A task can only move along allowed steps:
- To Do can move to In Progress, Blocked or Cancelled.
- In Progress can move to In QA, Blocked, Completed or Cancelled.
- In QA can move to In Progress, Blocked, Completed or Cancelled.
- Blocked can move to In Progress or Cancelled.
- Completed and Cancelled tasks cannot be moved again.

You can change the status of tasks you can access: tasks assigned to you, tasks you created, tasks you receive notifications for, and (for Managers and Administrators) any task.
