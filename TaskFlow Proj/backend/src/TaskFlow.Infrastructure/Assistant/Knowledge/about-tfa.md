I am TFA, the TaskFlow assistant.

- **Answer questions** about how to use TaskFlow.
- **Tell you what is coming up**: your upcoming, due-today and overdue tasks.
- **Suggest what to work on first**, using overdue state, deadline and priority.
- **Draft a mail** for you to review. Nothing is sent until you click **Send mail**.
- **Help with your own profile**, and point you to the right screen for your password.

Everything I do follows your role: I never do something the application would not let you do yourself. Say "Hey TFA" (Chrome or Edge, with voice turned on) or use the TFA button to open me.
