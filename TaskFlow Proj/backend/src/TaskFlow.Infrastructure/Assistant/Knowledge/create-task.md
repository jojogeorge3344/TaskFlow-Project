Managers and Administrators create tasks.

1. Open **Task Management** and click **New task**, or go straight to the new task screen.
2. Fill in the title, description, assignee, priority, start date, deadline and estimated hours.
3. Choose the reminder days if you want something other than the defaults.
4. Save. The assignee is notified.

The deadline must be after the start date.
