Every task has a discussion and an activity history.

- **Comments:** open the task and choose **Discussion**. Write your comment and post it. The other people on the task are notified.
- **Activity history:** open the task and choose **Activity**. It lists status changes, reassignments, deadline changes and comments, with who did them and when.
