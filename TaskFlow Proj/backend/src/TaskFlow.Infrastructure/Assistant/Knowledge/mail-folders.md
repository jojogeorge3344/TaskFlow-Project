Open **Mail** to see your folders: Inbox, Sent, Drafts, Starred, Important and Trash.

- Click a mail to read it. Reading marks it as read.
- Use **Reply**, **Reply all** or **Forward** at the top of the mail. Reminder mails from TaskFlow cannot be replied to, but can be forwarded.
- Star or mark mails as important to find them quickly.
- **Trash** moves a mail out of your folders; open Trash to restore it or delete it forever.
