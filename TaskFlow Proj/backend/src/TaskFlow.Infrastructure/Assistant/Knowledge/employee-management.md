Administrators open **Employee Management** under TaskFlow Management.

- **Add** an employee with name, username, email, role, project and a starting password.
- **Edit** an employee to change their details, role or project, or to set a new password.
- **Deactivate** an employee who has left: they can no longer sign in, and can be reactivated later.
