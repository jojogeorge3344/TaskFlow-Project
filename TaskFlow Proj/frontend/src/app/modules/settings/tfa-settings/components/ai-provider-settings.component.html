These are the models TFA can answer with. Use this model switches TFA to it; add a model, or change one you added, on the Add AI model screen. Google Gemini is the default and has a free tier with limits; get a free key in Google AI Studio. Ollama is optional: it runs on your own server and is free. OpenAI GPT has no free tier: it only works with a paid API key. Gemini and OpenAI are cloud services: a user's question, related task details and matching TaskFlow document text are sent to them.

Document search needs a Google Gemini API key, whichever model answers. TaskFlow document text is sent to Google to be indexed, and each question is sent to Google to search it. Without a saved Gemini key, TFA cannot answer from the documents. On the free tier, Google may use what is sent to improve its products.
