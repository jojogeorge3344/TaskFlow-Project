## 1. Module Overview
The Legal module publishes TaskFlow's five legal documents (Copyright Notice, Terms of Use, Privacy Policy, Disclaimer and Third-Party Licence Notices). It also shows the copyright footer at the bottom of the sign-in screen, the account recovery screen and every screen inside the application. It is a small, read-only module: nobody enters or changes data here.

## 2. Purpose / Business Value
- Makes clear who owns TaskFlow ("© 2026 Jojo George. All Rights Reserved.") and what users may and may not do with it.
- Tells users what data TaskFlow keeps, where it is stored, and when assistant questions are sent to an outside AI provider.
- Credits the open-source components TaskFlow is built on and states their licences.
- Uses a single owner/contact record, so the footer, the documents and the browser page details always show the same owner name, year and contact email.

## 4. Screens in This Module

| # | Screen Name | Menu Path | Purpose | Visible To |
|---|-------------|-----------|---------|------------|
| 1 | Copyright Notice | Footer → **Copyright** (`/legal/copyright`) | Ownership, what is protected, what you may and may not do, how to ask for permission | Everyone, signed in or not |
| 2 | Terms of Use | Footer → **Terms of Use** (`/legal/terms`) | The conditions for using TaskFlow, including accounts, acceptable use, AI providers, speech input and governing law (India) | Everyone, signed in or not |
| 3 | Privacy Policy | Footer → **Privacy Policy** (`/legal/privacy`) | What data is collected and why, where it is stored, who can see what, security measures, retention and user rights | Everyone, signed in or not |
| 4 | Disclaimer | Footer → **Disclaimer** (`/legal/disclaimer`) | "As is" terms, limits on assistant output and suggestions, availability, limitation of liability | Everyone, signed in or not |
| 5 | Third-Party Licence Notices | Footer → **Licences** (`/legal/licenses`) | TaskFlow's own licence and a table of third-party components with their licences | Everyone, signed in or not |
| 6 | Legal footer (part of other screens) | Bottom of Sign in, Account Recovery and all in-app screens | Ownership line plus links to the five documents | Everyone |

There is no side-menu entry for the legal pages. They are reached only from the footer or by typing the address.

## 7. Related Modules
- **Authentication** – the Sign in and Account Recovery screens show the legal footer (dark style), so visitors can read the documents before they sign in.
- **Dashboard** – the back link on a legal page returns a signed-in user to their dashboard (`/admin`, `/manager` or `/me`).
- **All in-app modules** – the application shell shows the legal footer below every signed-in screen.
- **TaskFlow Assistant (TFA)** and **Settings (TFA Settings)** – the Terms and Privacy documents describe how assistant questions go to cloud or local AI models and that provider keys are encrypted.
- **Communication (Mail)** – the Privacy Policy states that each user sees only their own mail.

## 8. Expected Outcome
Anyone, signed in or not, can read TaskFlow's Copyright Notice, Terms of Use, Privacy Policy, Disclaimer and Licence Notices from the footer on any main screen. The ownership statement "© 2026 Jojo George. All Rights Reserved." looks the same on every screen and document, and in the browser page details.
