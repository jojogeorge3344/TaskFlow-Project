## 1. Module Overview
The Management module (menu **TaskFlow Management**) is where the organization's master data is kept: the people who use TaskFlow (employees and their roles), the departments they belong to, and the projects they work on together with the position and percentage of time each person gives to a project. Tasks, dashboards, workload, reports and the TaskFlow Assistant all rely on this data.

## 2. Purpose / Business Value
- Gives administrators one place to create sign-in accounts, give roles, switch accounts on or off and remove people who have left.
- Groups people into departments and projects. A person's managers come from these groups, so no one has to keep a separate "reports to" list.
- Stops people being over-booked: one person can be given at most 100% across all projects, and every project needs a manager.
- Protects the system. The last active Administrator cannot be removed, nobody can delete their own account, and anyone whose access changes is signed out straight away.

## 3. Who Can Access This Module

| Role          | Can See Module | Can Use Module | Access Level (Own / Team / Organization) |
|---------------|:--------------:|:--------------:|------------------------------------------|
| Administrator | ✅ | ✅ | Organization |
| Manager       | ✅ (Project Management only) | View only (projects, project details) | Organization (no team limit on the lists) |
| Employee      | ❌ | ❌ | – |
| Custom Role   | Configurable | Configurable | As granted (Organization) |

> Managers hold `employees.view` and `departments.view` by default. These let other screens list people and departments, such as the task assignee picker. They do **not** show the Employee Management or Department Management menu. Those menus need at least one action permission (create, edit, delete, and so on).

## 6. Permission Summary (Module Level)
The module has 18 permission keys of its own, and Employee Management also uses the Settings key `roles.administrator.assign`. The full table with role columns is in each child document.

| Child Module | Permission Keys | Administrator | Manager | Employee | Custom Role |
|--------------|-----------------|:-------------:|:-------:|:--------:|:-----------:|
| 03.1 Employee Management | `employees.view` | Granted | Granted | Denied | Configurable |
| 03.1 Employee Management | `employees.create`, `employees.edit`, `employees.status.change`, `employees.role.assign`, `employees.mfa.reset`, `employees.delete` | Granted | Denied | Denied | Configurable |
| 03.1 Employee Management | `roles.administrator.assign` (Settings key, used here) | Granted | Denied | Denied | Denied (not assignable) |
| 03.2 Department Management | `departments.view` | Granted | Granted | Denied | Configurable |
| 03.2 Department Management | `departments.create`, `departments.edit`, `departments.delete`, `departments.members.manage` | Granted | Denied | Denied | Configurable |
| 03.3 Project Management | `projects.view` | Granted | Granted | Denied | Configurable |
| 03.3 Project Management | `projects.create`, `projects.edit`, `projects.delete`, `projects.members.manage`, `positions.create` | Granted | Denied | Denied | Configurable |

Every key except `employees.view`, `projects.view` and `departments.view` also needs the view key of its screen. Older roles may still hold `employees.manage`, `projects.manage` or `departments.manage`. These act like the finer keys they were split into.

Some screens quietly need keys of a sibling child: the Employee Management screen loads projects and departments (`projects.view`, `departments.view`), and the department members dialog and project team picker load people (`employees.view`).

## 7. Related Modules
- **Authentication** – accounts created here are the sign-in accounts. Deactivating, deleting, or changing a username or role ends sessions, and the MFA reset is part of MFA set-up.
- **Monitoring (Tasks, Board, Workload)** – task assignees and recipients come from the employee list, every task belongs to a project, and workload uses project allocations. An employee with unfinished tasks cannot be deleted.
- **Dashboard** – a manager's team (departments they manage plus projects they are flagged on) decides what the manager dashboard shows. The organization dashboard counts employees, departments and projects.
- **Settings → Role Configuration** – the roles offered in the employee form, and the permission keys listed here, are managed there.
- **Communication** – the MFA reset sends the employee a system notification.
- **Reports** – reports filter by project and keep showing deleted people's history.
- **TaskFlow Assistant (TFA)** – the assistant can prepare a role change ("propose_role_change") that an administrator confirms. It then uses the same employee update.
- **Profile** – people change their own password and contact details there, not in Employee Management.

## 8. Expected Outcome
Administrators keep an accurate list of employees, departments and projects. Every person has the right role, belongs to the right departments, and is allocated to projects without passing 100%. Each project has a manager. Accounts can be switched off or removed safely without losing history, and access changes take effect at once. Managers can see the projects and teams they need when they plan and assign work.
