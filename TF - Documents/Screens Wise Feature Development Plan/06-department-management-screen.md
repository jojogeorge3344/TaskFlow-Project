# SCREEN 6: Department Management Screen

> Source: BRD for Task management.docx · TaskFlow – Intelligent Task Allocation & Tracking System · v1.0 · §2.6

**Screen Name:** Department Management
**Access:** Administrator

## Purpose

Organize employees according to their departments.

## Screen Elements

- Department list.
- Department name.
- Department description.
- Add department button.
- Edit department button.
- Department employee count.

## Functional Requirements

1. Allow administrators to create departments.
2. Allow administrators to edit department details.
3. Display employees associated with each department.
4. Allow filtering employees by department.
5. Prevent duplicate department names where appropriate.
6. Prevent deletion of departments that still have associated employees unless those relationships are handled safely.

## Expected Result

Employees can be organized into departments for easier administration and reporting.
