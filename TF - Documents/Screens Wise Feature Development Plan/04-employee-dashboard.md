# SCREEN 4: Employee Dashboard

> Source: BRD for Task management.docx · TaskFlow – Intelligent Task Allocation & Tracking System · v1.0 · §2.4

**Screen Name:** Employee Dashboard
**Access:** Employee

## Purpose

Provide employees with a personalized view of their work.

## Screen Elements

- My total tasks.
- My pending tasks.
- My tasks due today.
- My overdue tasks.
- My completed tasks.
- Upcoming deadlines list.
- Recent notifications.
- Recently updated tasks.

## Functional Requirements

1. Display only tasks the employee is authorized to access.
2. Show tasks assigned to the logged-in employee.
3. Highlight tasks with approaching deadlines.
4. Highlight overdue tasks.
5. Display task priority, deadline, and status.
6. Allow the employee to open a task's details.
7. Display recent notifications.
8. Provide shortcuts to view tasks and update progress.

## Expected Result

Employees can easily understand what work they need to complete and which deadlines require attention.
