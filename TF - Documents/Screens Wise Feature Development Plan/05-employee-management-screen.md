# SCREEN 5: Employee Management Screen

> Source: BRD for Task management.docx · TaskFlow – Intelligent Task Allocation & Tracking System · v1.0 · §2.5

**Screen Name:** Employee Management
**Access:** Administrator

## Purpose

Allow administrators to manage employee accounts.

## Screen Elements

- Employee list.
- Search field.
- Department filter.
- Role filter.
- Account status filter.
- Add employee button.
- Edit button.
- Activate/deactivate action.
- Employee details view.

## Functional Requirements

1. Display employee name, email, department, role, and account status.
2. Allow administrators to create employee accounts.
3. Allow administrators to update employee details.
4. Assign employees to departments.
5. Assign appropriate roles.
6. Allow administrators to activate or deactivate accounts.
7. Prevent duplicate registered email addresses.
8. Allow searching and filtering of employee records.
9. Prevent accidental deletion of employees who have related task records.

## Expected Result

The administrator can maintain accurate employee information and control account access.
